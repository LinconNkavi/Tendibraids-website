About ClubBraids by Theodore

Craftsmanship, hair health, and welcoming braiding services rooted in Sharpeville, Vaal.

Business Background

ClubBraids by Theodore is a vibrant hair braiding business owned by Theodore Moleboheng Senokoane, based in Sharpeville, Vaal. Specializing in intricate and creative braiding techniques, ClubBraids caters to individuals looking for professional and personalized hair styling services.

Theodore has become a trusted name in her community, known for delivering beautiful braids that embrace cultural heritage while providing clients with modern, trendy styles.

Theodore Moleboheng Senokoane — Founder

We Offer

  • Box Braids
  • Cornrows
  • Twists
  • Customized Hairstyles

Whether you are a woman, man, or child, ClubBraids has the expertise to meet your braiding needs. Theodore also offers personalized hair care consultations, recommending styles that promote hair health while ensuring your braids are both stylish and long-lasting.

For added convenience, ClubBraids provides mobile services for clients who prefer the comfort of at-home braiding sessions.

More Information

  • A deposit of R100 is required when booking your appointments.
  • Appointments in advance are possible through the online booking system or via phone.
  • A 24-hour cancellation policy applies to all bookings.
  • ClubBraids uses high-quality, approved hair care products.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide top-quality hair braiding services for all ages and styles while fostering confidence, neatness, and healthy hair through every braid and twist we create. We aim to foster confidence and beauty through every braid and twist we create.
